\begin{frame}{Bayesian Network vs factor graph}

    Bayesian network: factors are implicitly represented as edges, and variables are connected to their parents.

    Factor graph: factors are explicitly represented as nodes, and variables are connected to the factors they appear in.

    To get the factor graph from the Bayesian network, we can introduce a factor node for each stochastic site, and connect it to the variable node of that site and to the variable nodes of its parents.

\end{frame}
